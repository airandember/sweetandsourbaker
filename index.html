Starter to oven

Real sourdough takes its time.

Starter, shape, score, bake. Here is how a loaf comes together in Tiana’s kitchen.

  1. 01

    Starter

    It starts with a jar of bubbly sourdough starter, fed and waiting.

  2. 02

    Shape

    The dough is folded and shaped into a round by hand.

  3. 03

    Score

    A blade cuts the pattern, so the loaf opens where it should.

  4. 04

    Bake

    Into a hot oven. The score bursts open and the crust turns gold.

  5. 05

    Cool

    Out of the oven and onto the rack to cool before it’s bagged.
